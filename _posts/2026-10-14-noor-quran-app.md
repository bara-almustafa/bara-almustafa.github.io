# Noor Quran — Adhan & Qibla

**Platforms:** Android 10+ · iPhone iOS 16+ (sideload) · **Price:** Free, no ads, no account

I built Noor Quran, a Quran and prayer app with recitations, the mushaf, prayer times with
the adhan, and a Qibla compass, available in 21 languages.

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest release](https://github.com/bara-almustafa/noor-quran/releases/latest)**

- **Android:** download `NoorQuran-1.0.0.apk` on your phone, open it, and allow
  "install unknown apps" if asked.
- **iPhone:** download `NoorQuran-1.0.0-unsigned.ipa` and install it with
  [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) or [AltStore](https://altstore.io) using your Apple ID
  (with a free Apple ID it needs re-signing every 7 days).

Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt` in the release.

## Features

**Listen**
- 240+ reciters with every riwayah and moshaf from mp3quran.net
- Play all, shuffle, queue, repeat, playback speed, sleep timer
- Background playback with lock-screen controls, and offline downloads
- 170+ Quran radio stations, live Quran & Sunnah TV, videos, audio tafsir

**Read**
- Full Quran text (Uthmani script) with translations in dozens of languages
- Read-along: each ayah lights up as it is recited
- Mushaf pages, adjustable font size, verse of the day, text tafsir

**Pray**
- Prayer times for your location with 23 calculation methods
- The full adhan at each prayer time, switchable per prayer
- Hijri date and a live countdown to the next prayer
- Qibla compass: a big arrow pointing to the Kaaba, updated live from GPS

**Remember**
- Hisn al-Muslim adhkar with counters and audio, hadith collections, tasbih

**Languages:** Arabic, English, French, Russian, German, Spanish, Turkish, Chinese, Thai, Urdu,
Bengali, Bosnian, Uyghur, Persian, Tajik, Malayalam, Tagalog, Indonesian, Portuguese, Hausa
and Swahili.

## Privacy

No ads, no account, no tracking. Location is used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla
direction. Read the [privacy policy]({{ '/noor-privacy/' | relative_url }}).
