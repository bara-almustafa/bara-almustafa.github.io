# Noor Quran – Adhan & Qibla: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

This policy explains how the **Noor Quran – Adhan & Qibla** app for Android and iOS ("the app")
handles your information. The app is developed by Bara Almustafa.

## Summary

- No account, sign-in, advertising or analytics.
- The app does not collect personal data on any server of its own — it has none.
- Your location is used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.

## Location

If you allow location access, the app reads your device location while you use it to:

- **Prayer times:** your latitude and longitude are sent to the
  [Aladhan prayer-times API](https://aladhan.com/prayer-times-api) (api.aladhan.com), which
  calculates the times and returns them. If you turn off "Use My Location" and type a city instead,
  only the city and country names are sent.
- **Qibla:** the direction to the Kaaba is calculated entirely on your device.

Location is never used in the background, never sold, and never used for advertising.
You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings and enter a city manually.

## Notifications

Prayer-time notifications and the adhan are scheduled locally on your device. No notification
data is sent anywhere.

## Content services

The app streams and downloads Quran recitations, text, translations, tafsir, hadith, adhkar, radio
and video from these public services. Like any website, they receive standard request information
(such as your IP address and device type) when the app contacts them:

- [mp3quran.net](https://www.mp3quran.net) – recitations, radio, live TV, videos, tafsir, ayah timings
- [Al Quran Cloud](https://alquran.cloud) / cdn.islamic.network – Quran text, translations, ayah audio
- [QuranEnc](https://quranenc.com) – translations with commentary
- [Hisn al-Muslim](https://www.hisnmuslim.com) – adhkar
- hadis-api-id.vercel.app – hadith collections
- data-rosy.vercel.app – additional radio stations

Each service handles that information under its own privacy policy.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, theme, prayer method, favorites, last read and listened position, tasbih
count) and any recitations you download are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app
deletes them.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
